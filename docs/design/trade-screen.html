What this mockup is asserting

  • Three columns, one direction of travel. Your inventory on the left, what you are giving in the middle, what you are getting on the right. Click a row to move it into the offer; click it again to pull it back.
  • Cyan is yours, red is theirs. The game already uses cyan for equipped and active, red for structure — so the side you control reads as cyan without inventing a new colour.
  • Any change clears both confirmations. Server-side rule, surfaced here rather than discovered. It is the one thing that stops a swap-at-the-last-moment scam, and it is invisible in the chat version.
  • Eddies are a first-class row, not an item. They are gold, they sit under the items on both sides, and only your side has steppers.
  • The weight readout stays visible. A trade that would put you over 200 has to be refusable before both sides confirm, not after.
  • This replaces /trade item <id> <quantity> — a flow that needs /inventory first just to learn an id you can paste.
