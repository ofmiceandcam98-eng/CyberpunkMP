What changed from the first pass, and why

  • It is an overlay, not a full-screen menu. First version was an inventory-style screen taking the whole display. This one sits beside chat, because that is where the conversation that starts a trade already is — and because a full-screen menu during an RP scene takes you out of it.
  • Two columns, not three. At real size there is no room for a separate inventory column. Picking an item comes from the row list on your side; the inventory it draws from is the one the game already has.
  • Anchored to the same bottom line as chat — 200 game-pixels up, matching margin(60, 0, 0, 200). The two panels read as one strip rather than two floating windows.
  • Weight and eddies stay in the header. A trade that would put you over 200 has to be refusable before both sides confirm.
  • Chat stays live behind it. Trades get negotiated in text; hiding the conversation to complete the deal would be the wrong trade-off.
  • Sizes here are honest but not authoritative — the 60 and 200 margins are read from the code; the panel's own 900×470 is a proposal, and the real one wants checking against a 1920×1080 capture in game.
