# Weekly - 23 Sep 2026

## Summary
Night City Online is very much still in development, and from now on you will get one of
these every week - what we worked on, what actually works, and what still does not. No
hype, no promises we cannot keep.

The last launcher release was v0.3.123 on 13 September, so it has been quiet on your end
for ten days. It has not been quiet on ours. Most of this week went into the character
system and the join flow, plus a large cleanup that will make future updates land faster.

## What we fixed this week
These are fixed and waiting in the build we are testing. They reach you with the next
launcher release - the forced update described below - so they are not in your game yet.

- Connecting now stops at the character selector instead of dropping you into the world as
  whoever you played last. That was the bug where the picker appeared several seconds late,
  on top of a world you were already standing in.
- On a cold start the main menu sometimes had no CONNECT button at all, and only a relaunch
  fixed it. The menu now waits for the mod to finish loading instead of giving up.
- Your character no longer keeps gear it never chose. A spawn straight after a world reload
  could leave the starter template's equipped items on you permanently.
- Eddies are no longer wiped on a spawn the client could not identify as a brand new
  character.
- New characters get their starter kit on the first save rather than the second.
- Returning to the character screen now properly releases your character, which was behind
  a whole family of "it refuses to switch / delete / change my look" problems.
- New characters are moved out of the starting holding room instead of spawning in the box.

## What works right now
- The launcher: one click to play, with Discord sign-in and roles.
- Making a character, customising it, and having it load as yours next time.
- The server world, chat, voice chat, and other players moving around you.
- Vehicles, including other people driving them.
- Emote wheel and the in-game phone.

## What does not work yet
Being straight with you about this, because a few of these look finished and are not.
- **Player versus player combat is not validated.** The fixes are written and two of us
  have never actually had a firefight to prove it. Until we do, treat PvP as untested.
- **Trading has no clickable interface yet.** The panel renders and the actions currently
  run as typed commands. The mouse cursor half is still being built.
- **Vehicles have known sync problems** - getting out can make you appear to jump a long
  way on someone else's screen, and there is a crash we are chasing when you enter a car
  near another player.
- **Money persistence needs one more live check.** The fix is in; it has not been confirmed
  with a real session yet.

## What is coming
- A public update that needs the client and server to change together. When it lands you
  **must** update through the launcher, or you will not be able to connect. We will announce
  the window before it happens.
- A clickable trade screen.
- A proper two-player test pass so the combat work stops being theoretical.

## Known issues
- CET (Cyber Engine Tweaks) is still listed as incompatible while we re-test it. The reason
  for the original conflict has been removed, so this may be lifted shortly.
- If the main menu ever comes up without CONNECT, relaunch once - the fix for that is in the
  next release, not the one you have.
