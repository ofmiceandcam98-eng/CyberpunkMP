# How you can help — current as of 2026-09-04 (v0.3.113)

**Legend** — 🧍 alone · 👥 needs two people · 🚗 needs four

Everything below is BUILT and waiting on humans. Your session logs ship to the server
automatically when you play — no need to post files unless asked.

## 👥 The one thing that helps most

**Two people in the world at once on the current launcher.** Walk, shoot each other,
trade (`/trade`), call each other (`/call`), go down and get revived. Every one of those
systems is finished and none has ever seen two humans at once. Say in Discord when you
are on so the logs get read against a known session.

## 🚗 Needs four bodies, once

Vehicle seats: four people in one car (a fifth must be refused, not crammed in).

## 🧍 Helps any time

- Drive around for a few minutes — highway, downtown grid, parking, stop-and-go. Real
  routes tune the vehicle sync.
- Found money that vanished on relog? Say so with the time — the money instrumentation
  is live and has never yet caught the bug in the act.
- Any crash: just relaunch and mention it. The logs already made the trip.
